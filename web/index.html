HOW TO PLAY

Objective: Protect the central Dzong fortress from incoming waves of molten red slimes attacking along 4 paths!

Building: Click on circular build spots () around paths to construct defense towers using Gold.

Upgrading: Click on any built tower to open its upgrade panel for higher damage & stats.

Guard Towers: Guard Towers summon armed Maroon Warriors with curved swords directly onto the road to block slimes in combat!

Waves: Press 'START BATTLE' when you're ready to spawn enemies. If slimes reach the Dzong base, you lose health!
